Music

Guitar

I’ve practised guitar for ten years, playing electric and acoustic guitar in school ensembles, alongside instruments such as the sarangi, and as lead guitarist at annual day. Encouraged by my teacher, Kuldeepak Sir, I began teaching other students and later carried that experience into Musica Universalis.

Performances & rehearsals

MusicaUniversalis

President (2025) · Volunteer (2023–2024)

I began by teaching guitar, developed accessible digital lessons as technology head, and later became president. My work now combines teaching, instrument support and partner coordination to make free music education available beyond individual sessions.

Foundations of Sound offers weekly music-literacy classes for ages 6–18, supported by instrument donations. For sustained study, the programme provides coaching and performance preparation for Trinity College London and Bhatkhande examinations, with more than 600 students enrolled.

Our team of more than 25 works with five NGOs and a music conservatoire, including Impact, Naya Savera, Aashray and Soul of Symphony. World Without Walls connects chapters in India, China, Ukraine and London through shared compositions and a newsletter; the programme reports reaching more than 5,000 people through education, events and publication.

SoulCare offers music-therapy sessions supported by licensed therapists for children experiencing anxiety or trauma, alongside the programme’s teaching and performance work.

We worked with Aashray Care Home to organise Aashray Fest, giving students an opportunity to perform for the wider community. The event was supported by the Government of Rajasthan and attended by eight Rajasthan government officials.
