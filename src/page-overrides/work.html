Human Archive

Business Growth & Machine Learning Data Intern · 18 May–29 June 2026

3D hand trajectories for robot-learning datasets

When a robot learns to pick up an object from a human demonstration, it needs the hand’s three-dimensional path and the timing of contact as well as the visible action. Ordinary video alone does not directly supply these movement and force measurements.

At Human Archive, I developed 3D hand-movement representations for robot-learning datasets. The paired demonstration shows the recorded physical task beside its reconstructed hand trajectory.

Data quality and robot trials

I synchronised video, depth, motion and tactile streams and checked their quality before dataset use. I also trained and evaluated robots on manipulation tasks, using failed grasps and incomplete actions to revise demonstrations. The hand representations contributed to products supplied to six AI laboratories.

Factory partnerships

During the six-week hybrid internship, I managed partnerships with 12 manufacturing facilities and helped onboard more than 100 workers for real-task collection.

Wink

Understanding visitor movement from store footage

Although store cameras record shoppers, retailers still need to establish which displays attract attention and where visitors spend time. To obtain these measurements from existing footage, I developed Wink, which tracks visits and summarises activity within the store.

Wink combines YOLO detection, multi-object tracking and person re-identification to follow shoppers through existing store-camera footage. Point-in-polygon tests assign positions to store zones; entry and exit times measure dwell time.

Behavioural measurements

The dashboard brings together visitor counts, stopping locations, dwell time and movement between zones. Consistent visitor identities prevent repeated frames from being counted as new visits.

Edge deployment

I moved inference onto retailers’ existing computers, reducing reported monthly operating costs from approximately $500 to $5 while keeping raw footage inside the store.
