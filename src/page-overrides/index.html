Here’s an ambulance-routing system I built to coordinate traffic signals.

Project details

An ambulance cannot gain much from a green light if the road ahead is already full. I developed AmbuGo to plan both the route and selective signal changes, shortening the journey without shifting the delay onto other drivers.

In matched simulations, selective priority retained about 94% of aggressive control’s ambulance-time saving while reducing excess traffic accumulation. Separately, a 31-intersection Punjab pilot preceded expansion to 121 intersections.
